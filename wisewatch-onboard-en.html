WiseWatch lets you talk to Google Gemini by voice on your Apple Watch. Leave your phone in your pocket — just talk to your watch.

3 Steps to Start

  1. Set up a Gemini API key
    Free with any Google account. How to get & set it up.
  2. Talk on your Apple Watch
    Open WiseWatch on your Apple Watch, tap the mic button, and just ask. Learn more about input methods (Apple).
  3. Add it to your watch face (optional)
    Add the complication to launch WiseWatch with one tap. Setup steps (Apple).

What you say is processed on Google's Gemini API servers. Please also see things to know about using generative AI.

How to Use

  • Microphone button: Tap and speak — Gemini answers.
  • Speaker button: Toggle spoken replies on or off.

Pricing

WiseWatch uses the Gemini API's free tier, so there's no monthly subscription. There are some limits, such as requests-per-minute. See details here.
